## What judges score

- **Builders onboarded** — traditional-tech agentic devs brought onto Solana
- **Onchain volume** — real volume the agent/product does on Solana during the run
- **Attention garnered** — streams, clips, audience
- **$ANSEM volume** — bonus for a net-new $ANSEM use case shown live on stream
- **Deploy early** — projects live sooner get watched longer

**Stream format:** 15 minutes, 4 questions: (1) founder & team background, (2) product, demo, problem, (3) market, GTM, traction, (4) token utility, roadmap, vision.
